Steak

Ingredients

  • 2 ribeye steaks (1 inch thick)
  • Salt and pepper to taste
  • 2 tablespoons olive oil
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 2 sprigs fresh rosemary
  • 2 tablespoons unsalted butter

Instructions

  1. Take the steaks out of the refrigerator and let them come to room temperature for about 30 minutes.
  2. Preheat a cast-iron skillet over high heat until it is smoking hot.
  3. Season both sides of the steaks generously with salt and pepper.
  4. Add olive oil to the skillet and place the steaks in the pan. Sear for 3-4 minutes on each side for medium-rare, or until desired doneness is reached.
  5. Add minced garlic, rosemary, and butter to the skillet. Spoon the melted butter over the steaks while cooking for added flavor.
  6. Remove the steaks from the skillet and let them rest for 5 minutes before slicing. Serve and enjoy!
