Bun Cha

Ingredients

  • 1 lb ground pork
  • 1/4 cup fish sauce
  • 2 tablespoons sugar
  • 1 tablespoon minced garlic
  • 1 tablespoon minced shallots
  • 1/2 teaspoon black pepper
  • 1/4 teaspoon salt
  • 1/4 cup chopped fresh herbs (mint, cilantro, and Thai basil)
  • 1/4 cup chopped peanuts (optional)
  • 1/4 cup rice vinegar
  • 1/4 cup water
  • 1 tablespoon sugar
  • 1 tablespoon fish sauce
  • 1 clove garlic, minced

Instructions

  1. In a large bowl, combine the ground pork, fish sauce, sugar, garlic, shallots, black pepper, and salt. Mix well until all ingredients are evenly incorporated.
  2. Form the pork mixture into small patties, about 2 inches in diameter. Set aside.
  3. In a small bowl, whisk together the rice vinegar, water, sugar, fish sauce, and minced garlic to make the dipping sauce. Adjust the seasoning to taste.
  4. Preheat a grill or grill pan over medium-high heat. Grill the pork patties for about 3-4 minutes per side, or until cooked through and nicely charred.
  5. Serve the grilled pork patties with the dipping sauce, fresh herbs, and chopped peanuts on the side. Enjoy!
